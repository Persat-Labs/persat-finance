How do I get started with Persat?

Join the waitlist below. As testnet approaches, early members get first access to borrow against their Bitcoin or lend stablecoins — with priority by position on the list.

Who holds my assets during a loan?

No one but the program. Bitcoin collateral and stablecoin loans sit in smart-contract escrow on Solana — Persat never takes custody of anything.

What can I borrow against?

Bitcoin. Lock it as collateral, borrow stablecoins against it on agreed terms, and repay any time to unlock it — no selling, no custodian.

What can I lend?

Stablecoins. Lend into terms you agree with directly, secured by the borrower’s locked Bitcoin — enforced automatically by smart contracts.

When is testnet?

Soon. Waitlist members are notified first, region by region, the moment testnet opens — followed by mainnet later this cycle.
