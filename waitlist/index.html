How_It_Works

A credit marketplace and trust layer

People around the world hold valuable Bitcoin but are forced to sell it, or hand it to a custodian, the moment they need real cash. Persat Finance gives two willing parties the infrastructure to safely agree, secure, and settle a loan — Bitcoin as collateral, stablecoins as the loan itself — enforced automatically by smart contracts. No company holds your assets at any point.
