Discovery Without

  • Same enforcement — discovery is how you meet; smart contracts are how the agreement holds.

Frequently Asked
Questions

Infrastructure for trust-minimized peer-to-peer lending — secured by digital assets, enforced by smart contracts, starting with Bitcoin.

How do I get started with Persat?

Join the waitlist below. Early members get first access to the Bitcoin pilot — our first market for trust-minimized, collateral-backed lending — with priority by position on the list.

Who holds my assets during a loan?

The infrastructure holds the rules. Collateral and loans sit in smart-contract vaults on Solana — not company wallets. Confidence comes from enforcement, not from trusting Persat with your keys.

Why start with Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is our first collateral asset because holders already need liquidity without selling. Lock BTC, borrow against agreed terms, repay to unlock — while the broader infrastructure stays open to more assets later.

What is Persat building overall?

Infrastructure for lending and borrowing between people who do not need to personally trust one another — collateral-backed credit, programmable agreements, transparent enforcement. Bitcoin is the first vehicle on that highway.

When is testnet?

The Bitcoin pilot is live on Solana devnet for early access. Waitlist members are notified as access expands — mainnet only after the model is proven.
